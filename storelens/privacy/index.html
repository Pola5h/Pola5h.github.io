Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 • Developer: Kamruzzaman Polash
Short version: StoreLens does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any browsing history, personal data, or analytics. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser.

1. Data Collection

StoreLens operates entirely on-device. It does not use third-party analytics, crash trackers, remote telemetry, or advertising SDKs. No URLs you visit or store reports you generate are ever sent to our servers or any third party.

2. Browser Permissions Explained

  • activeTab — Grants temporary read access to the currently open tab only at the moment you click the StoreLens toolbar icon. StoreLens cannot read tabs in the background.
  • scripting — Runs a lightweight, read-only inspection script inside the active tab to detect public storefront markers (platform, theme, asset URLs, computed brand colors, fonts, and pixels).
  • storage — Saves your optional StoreLens Pro license key locally in your browser's extension storage so your Pro status persists between sessions.

3. Public Storefront Requests

When you click Load Products inside the Products tab, your browser directly requests the store's public catalog endpoint (such as /products.json on Shopify or /wp-json/wc/store/v1/products on WooCommerce). These requests go directly from your browser to the website you are inspecting.
