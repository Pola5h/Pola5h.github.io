04 — FAQ

Common questions before we start.

Do I need a technical specification before contacting you?+

No. Whether you are a non-technical business owner with an operational headache or a CTO with a detailed architecture brief, we can start right away. Just explain what your business needs in plain language, and I will translate it into a clear technical plan and milestone quote.

How are project scopes and pricing structured?+

Every project begins with a clear discovery step to define exact deliverables and milestones. Client projects are quoted as fixed-scope milestone phases, while ongoing engineering or team roles work on predictable monthly retainers—never surprise hourly bills.

Can you integrate with our existing WooCommerce, Shopify, or legacy PHP system?+

Yes. I specialize in connecting and stabilizing live systems—building custom WooCommerce–Laravel sync bridges, real-time POS inventory ledgers, Pathao/Steadfast courier automation, bKash/SSLCommerz/Stripe payment flows, and refactoring legacy PHP codebases without downtime.

Yes. Since 2022, I have shipped production platforms for clients in France, the UK, the US, and Bangladesh. All communication, code reviews, and documentation are in fluent English via Slack, GitHub, Linear, or WhatsApp with flexible time-zone overlap.

Who owns the source code and intellectual property?+

You own 100% of the source code, database migrations, Docker configs, and API documentation from day one. All work is committed directly to your repository with zero vendor lock-in or recurring license traps.

How fast can we start?+

I reply to all inquiries within 4 hours on business days, and most scoped projects or performance sprints can kick off within 48 to 72 hours.
